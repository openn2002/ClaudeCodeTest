## Company Overview

**Digital Wellness** is an Australian-founded health technology company that builds and operates
digital platforms for the world's most credible, science-backed weight loss programs. The company
has been operating for 15+ years and has positively impacted more than **3 million lives**.

Digital Wellness does not sell its own diet brand — it acts as the digital product and technology
engine behind two iconic, licensed health programs:

| Market | Program | Partner |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Australia | CSIRO Total Wellbeing Diet (TWD) | CSIRO (Australia's national science agency) |
| United States | Mayo Clinic Diet | Mayo Clinic |

The company also has enterprise (B2B) channels: private health insurers, pharmaceutical companies,
corporate wellness programs, and government health initiatives.

**Headquarters:** Australia (also operating in the US)
**Website:** https://www.digitalwellness.com/
**Founder:** Scott Penn

## Our Products

### 1. CSIRO Total Wellbeing Diet (Australia)
**URL:** https://www.totalwellbeingdiet.com/au/

The CSIRO TWD is Australia's most scientifically credible online weight loss program, originally
launched as a book series in 2005 and moved to a digital platform (co-developed with Digital
Wellness) in 2014.

**What it is:** A higher-protein, low-GI digital diet program backed by peer-reviewed CSIRO research.

**Key features:**
- Personalised meal plans (including the new Protein Balance Power plan)
- AI-powered weight loss coach called **Hope**
- **My Journey** — AI-powered personalised to-do list and guidance tool
- Food, exercise, and weight tracking tools (mobile app + web)
- 1:1 health coaching with accredited practising dietitians
- Group coaching sessions (free)
- Supportive member community (50,000+ members)
- CSIRO Total Wellbeing Diet cookbook (included)
- At-home exercise plans for all fitness levels
- Diet personality dashboard

**Fast Start (meal replacement — AU only):**
A companion product to the CSIRO TWD for members who want faster initial weight loss or a
simpler approach to managing cravings. Protein shake format. Sold in parallel with — not instead
of — the TWD program.

**Results:** On average, members lose **7.2% of body weight after 12 weeks** and maintain
weight loss for the year. Nearly 1 in 2 members who complete the program lose 5%+ of starting
body weight (clinically significant threshold).

**Pricing (AU):**
- 1-month plan: $79
- 12-week program: $299
- Loyalty plan (post-program): $19.95/month recurring
- Health insurance discounts: HCF (20% off), TUH ($30 off), select funds (12 weeks free)

**Published research:** One of the most academically published digital health platforms in the world,
with studies in peer-reviewed journals including a 5-year community cohort evaluation.

### 2. Mayo Clinic Diet (United States)
**URL:** https://diet.mayoclinic.org/us/

The Mayo Clinic Diet is a digitally delivered weight loss program built on the authority of the
Mayo Clinic brand — one of the most trusted medical institutions in the world.

**What it is:** A behaviour-change and nutrition program built around 15 sustainable habits,
designed to produce 6–10 lbs of weight loss in the first 2 weeks and lasting change long-term.

**Programs available (2025):**
- Core Mayo Clinic Diet program
- Heart Health Program (cholesterol, blood pressure)
- Diabetes Program (prediabetes, type 2 diabetes)
- Weight-Loss Medications Program (GLP-1 companion program)

**Key features:**
- 8 flexible meal plan types (Mediterranean, Healthy Keto, Higher Protein, Vegetarian, etc.)
- **Habit Optimizer** — tracks and coaches behaviour change
- **Diet Score** — 100-point daily scoring system built on 5 medically backed pillars
- Freestyle Meal Planning (swap, favourite, and customise meals)
- Unlimited group coaching with Mayo Clinic registered dietitians
- Digital tracking (food, weight, exercise, habits; barcode scanner; fitness device sync)
- Mobile app (iOS + Android)
- Access to Mayo Clinic clinician content and videos
- Private Facebook Group community
- **Nutrition Therapy** via Berry Street (board-certified RDs, most insurance accepted, ~$0 OOP)
- Clinical Membership tier: includes end-to-end GLP-1 medication management

**Results:** Members lose 6–10 lbs in the first 2 weeks. Rated in 10 of 11 Best Diets categories
by U.S. News & World Report. Named #1 Diet Program (2022). Backed by Mayo Clinic clinical expertise.

**Pricing (US):**
- $49.99/month standard
- Significant discounts available for upfront annual payment (up to 78% off)
- CareCredit financing available on purchases of $200+
- Clinical Membership pricing varies; includes GLP-1 medical management

## Target Audience

### Current Reality
- **Age:** 50+ skew (both AU and US markets)
- **Gender:** Female skew (based on historical data)
- **Psychographic:** People who are sick of yo-yo dieting; want credible, science-backed information
  rather than fads; value expert guidance; are willing to invest in their health; motivated by
  long-term health outcomes more than aesthetics

### Desired Direction
- Digital Wellness wants to attract a **younger demographic** (35–50) without abandoning the
  existing 50+ base
- The 50+ audience is not a problem to solve — it is a real, motivated, underserved group that
  responds well to science-backed messaging and should continue to be served well
- The stretch goal is expanding relevance into the 35–50 cohort, where GLP-1 interest and
  digital-first health behaviours are growing fast

### Audience Insight
Because both programs are grounded in institutional science (CSIRO, Mayo Clinic), the audience
self-selects for:
- Credibility-seeking behaviour ("I want proof this works")
- Trust in medical/scientific authority
- Frustration with previous diet failures
- Willingness to follow a structured, evidence-based approach
- Health-motivated (managing chronic conditions, reducing medication, improving energy) rather
  than purely appearance-motivated

## Strategic Context for Agents

When conducting research, creating content, or building strategies, agents should keep the
following in mind:

1. **Science is our moat.** Every piece of content should be traceable to credibility. CSIRO and
   Mayo Clinic are category-defining brand names. Use them deliberately — they are trust signals
   that no competitor can replicate.

2. **The 50+ audience is not a liability.** It is a motivated, underserved demographic with high
   intent and willingness to pay. Content and copy should never condescend or ignore this group.

3. **GLP-1 is a major strategic opportunity.** The Mayo Clinic Diet already has a dedicated
   Weight-Loss Medications Program. The CSIRO TWD has not yet built this out. Content that
   positions our programs as the ideal structure and support layer for GLP-1 users is a high-priority
   growth lever — particularly given our 50+ demographic skews toward the primary GLP-1 audience.

4. **Fast Start (AU) is a conversion and retention tool.** It serves members who need simplicity
   or faster initial results. It is not a standalone brand — it is a product that feeds people
   into the broader CSIRO TWD program and keeps them engaged.

5. **Community is a weakness relative to WW and Kic.** Our 50,000-member community is real but
   not a prominent marketing asset. Building community visibility and UGC is an opportunity.

6. **We operate in a regulated health environment.** All content claims must be supportable.
   Australia has TGA considerations for health products; the US has FTC guidelines. Avoid
   unsubstantiated superlatives; anchor claims to published research or program outcomes data.

7. **Enterprise / B2B is a second revenue stream.** Health insurers, pharma, corporate wellness,
   and government are existing channels. When relevant, consider B2B angles (LinkedIn, corporate
   wellness positioning) alongside D2C recommendations.
